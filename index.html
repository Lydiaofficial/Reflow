ReflowPDF → EPUB

Make a PDF readable on your e-reader.

Other converters keep the page layout, so the text lands on top of itself. Reflow rebuilds the paragraphs, chapters and figures instead.

Drop a PDF here or tap to choose one · nothing leaves this browser

How it works

  1. Drop in the PDFThe whole conversion runs on your device. There's no upload, no account and no size limit beyond your own memory.
  2. Check the previewYou read one chapter at a time, set the way your e-reader will set it. Reflow takes chapter names from the book's own contents page or bookmarks.
  3. Fix the contentsRename, reorder, merge or remove entries in the list. Click any line in the preview to promote it to a chapter heading, or click a heading to demote it.
  4. Download and side-loadYou get a standard EPUB 3 with a working table of contents, a cover and your images.

Text and images only — hand-set layout, tables and multi-column pages won't survive intact. A scanned PDF has no text layer at all and needs OCR before any converter can read it.
